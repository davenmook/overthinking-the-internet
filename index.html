About

I was a terrible student.

I started community college in remedial math, reading, and writing. To finish in four years I took 24 credit hours a semester, transferred to the University of Oklahoma, did undergraduate RF research, and ended up teaching Circuits 1, a class I'd failed three times.

Since then I've designed cell sites at T-Mobile, led RF engineering teams at DISH, and now, at Ookla, lead customer relationships with major US phone and internet companies, helping them see what their own customers actually experience. I still remember what it's like not to get the math, which is why I explain it the way I do.
